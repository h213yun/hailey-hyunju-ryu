I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at Korea University (Computer Vision Lab, advised by Sangpil Kim), focusing on reinforcement learning for character control, multimodal generative models, and image watermarking.

Previously, I received my B.E. in Engineering from Sungshin Women's University, where I focused on AI security and cloud security.
